# Third-party dictionary data

The additional Tigrinya dictionary entries in
`constants/qanqa-dictionary-thousand-source.json` are derived from the Tigrinya
edition of Wiktionary through the Kaikki machine-readable dictionary project.

- Source: https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Tigrinya/
- Wiktionary licensing: https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights
- Wiktextract project: https://github.com/tatuylonen/wiktextract

The source data is made available under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike license and the GNU Free Documentation License. Modified
entries retain those licensing terms. Kaikki's data was extracted from the
English Wiktionary dump dated August 5, 2026 and post-processed on August 28,
2026.

The additional entries in `constants/qanqa-dictionary-10k-source-*.json` are
derived from the Ge'ez Frontier Foundation Data Archive, principally its
Concise English–Tigrinya Dictionary dataset.

- Source archive: https://github.com/geezorg/data
- Project site: https://data.geez.org/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International

These derived entries retain the source archive's CC BY-SA 4.0 licensing terms.

The corpus-derived Tigrinya headwords and example sentences in
`constants/qanqa-dictionary-corpus-source-*.json` are extracted from
TIgrinyaLargeTextDataset by Mewael Tsegay Desta.

- Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/mewaeltsegay/TigrinyaLargeText
- License: MIT

The corpus entries preserve authentic example sentences. They intentionally do
not claim dictionary definitions, synonyms, or antonyms that the source does
not provide.
