Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Fidel mobile application (the “App”) handles information. Fidel is an educational app that provides Tigrinya learning activities, a dictionary, word reels, games, and translation tools.

Information we collect

Fidel does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone access, or payment information. We do not operate an advertising or analytics system in the App.

Translation and handwriting features

When you choose to use translation, the text you enter and the selected languages are sent over the internet to a Google translation service so the requested translation can be returned. When you use handwriting recognition, the handwriting input needed to recognize the characters is sent to a Google input service. Do not enter sensitive or personal information into these features. Google may process technical information, such as an IP address, as part of providing and securing its services. Google’s handling of information is governed by its own privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

App settings and learning interactions may be held locally on your device as needed for the App to function. Fidel does not currently upload this local information to a Fidel account or Fidel-operated cloud database. You can remove locally stored App information by deleting the App or clearing its storage through your device settings.

Children’s privacy

Fidel is designed as an educational resource and may be used by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through accounts, advertising, analytics, or profiling. A parent or guardian should supervise a child’s use of the translation and handwriting features and ensure that the child does not submit personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us so we can review the concern.

Sharing and selling information

We do not sell personal information. Information is transmitted to a third-party service only when needed to provide a feature you request, such as translation or handwriting recognition, or when required by law.

Security and retention

Internet transmissions cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure. Fidel does not intentionally retain translation text or handwriting input on a Fidel-operated server. Third-party providers may retain or process information according to their own policies.

Your choices

Translation and handwriting recognition are optional. You can use the App’s offline educational content without using those network features. You may also stop all App processing by closing or uninstalling the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email degenlogistics@gmail.com.
